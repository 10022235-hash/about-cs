Other Useful Information:

  • Data privacy is a balancing act between protecting your personal life and enjoying convenient, personalized technology.
  • Secondary privacy harms are the indirect, negative consequences that happen after your personal data is collected, leaked, or misused.
  • To protect yourself from secondary privacy harms, use strong passwords, turn on two-factor authentication, and limit what you share online.
